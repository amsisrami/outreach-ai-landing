No made-up findings

Every sentence traces back to a data point.

Generic AI tools invent "outdated design" and "weak messaging". Prospects can smell it. OutreachAI only reports what the scan actually measured, quotes the number, and translates it into business impact.

  • Grounded by design. The AI is forbidden from commenting on colours, layout or imagery it can't see.
  • Ranked by revenue. Lost leads first, then lost trust, lost traffic, speed — not by how technical the issue sounds.
  • Honest when the site is good. Fewer findings beat padded ones. Severity "low" is allowed.

FAQ

Questions agencies ask us

Does the AI make things up about the site?

No. Every finding must quote a measured data point from the scan, and the model is explicitly blocked from commenting on anything it can't see — colours, layout, imagery, "feel". If a site is in good shape, you get fewer findings, not padded ones.

Which websites does it work on?

Any public page you can open in Chrome: WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, hand-coded sites. The scan reads the rendered page, so it doesn't depend on the platform.

Can I change the tone or add my offer?

The email uses placeholders for your name and agency, and the pitch is short enough to edit in seconds. Custom voice and offer presets are on the Pro roadmap.

What do you store?

Your account email, and the URL and date of each audit so we can count your free credits. The page scan is used to write the pitch and isn't sold or shared.
